The upstream project states that its flag designs are public domain and are
sourced primarily from Wikimedia Commons. Original SVGs and their construction
references remain in the private/local source under data/source-flags/.
Flag Bag renders only the 195-country roster from The Mapfather. Rendered files
preserve natural proportions; identifying metadata is removed and structural
IDs are renamed. See docs/flag-assets.json for original and rendered hashes.
